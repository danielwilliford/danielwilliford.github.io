Problem

AI research is cheap to generate and easy to overtrust.

A useful research agent has to show what it checked, where claims came from, what contradicts them, and what still needs verification before anyone acts on the output.

System

Make the research trail inspectable.

The workflow validates source metadata, deduplicates inputs, queues hypotheses, records deterministic tool-trace events, builds an evidence ledger, critiques conclusions, scores a quality rubric, and writes a readable memo with limits and next steps.

Architecture

Research becomes an artifact trail.

01 QuestionFrame the decision, scope, and human boundary before the run starts.
02 ToolsCollect source material, dated context, comparison points, and source metadata.
03 LedgerNormalize source-specific claims into evidence rows with confidence, source anchors, and open questions.
04 MemoTurn validated rows into a readable memo with limitations, warning labels, and decision boundaries.
05 Decision gateLabel stale inputs, missing data, and required refresh steps before any human action.

Enterprise vendor evaluation frame

A representative packet evaluates whether a tool or vendor is suitable for a regulated workflow. The important public signal is not the private source corpus; it is the process for connecting claims to evidence.

  • Working question: Can this system satisfy the user workflow without creating hidden compliance, security, or support risk?
  • Evidence lanes: product docs, security posture, integration surface, pricing/operational constraints, failure modes, and user handoff requirements.
  • Risk pass: Which claims depend on vendor marketing, stale docs, missing policy, contradiction markers, or untested integration assumptions?
  • Human boundary: Do not promote to implementation until missing controls and unresolved evidence gaps are reviewed by a person.

Research result

The output is a decision checklist, not a command.

Rubric visible quality

Scores or recommendations are only useful when the rubric and deductions are visible beside them.

Evidence rows

Claims are tied to source anchors, dates, confidence, and open questions.

Diligence steps

The packet names what must be refreshed, tested, or verified before implementation.

Human owned

No purchase, rollout, or operational change is authorized by the research artifact alone.

Implementation evidence

The page shows the artifact contract directly.

The page exposes the same chain the system uses: source list, dedupe report, tool trace, methodology note, evidence ledger, hypothesis queue, critique result, quality rubric, readable memo, and a human decision boundary.

Sensitive implementation details are not part of the evidence chain. No local paths, credentials, proprietary source notes, private strategies, or session state are needed to evaluate the work.
