Forward deployed delivery

From workflow discovery to governed prototype.

Cost aware AI operations

Not every subtask needs a premium hosted model. The agentic harness pattern routes routine classification, extraction, decomposition, formatting, and recall work to lightweight local or internal workers, uses larger models for synthesis, reserves hosted models for difficult reasoning or elasticity, and keeps final verification with a human operator.
